# Supplier SLA — Nordwerk Precision GmbH (FY26)

Covers Component XK-7 rev C supply (see supplier_registry.md SUP-044).

- Standard lead time: 3 weeks; expedited 7 days at 12% surcharge.
- Late delivery penalty: **5% of order value per week**, capped at 20%.
- Quality bar: field failure rate below 0.5% per quarter; two consecutive
  misses trigger a joint review and second-source evaluation.
- Uptime of the ordering portal: 99.5% monthly, excluding planned maintenance.

Aldercroft Electrics remains unapproved for XK-7 under this SLA (no change).
