## Product X — Field Scanner Unit
Product X (SKU: MER-X100) is our flagship field scanner for warehouse inventory.
It integrates optical array OA-9 and logic component **XK-7** for signal timing.

Key specifications:
- Power draw: 14W nominal, 22W peak.
- Operating range: -20C to 55C.
- Firmware: Helios 4.2 or later required for XK-7 timing calibration.
- Warranty: 36 months, on-site replacement within 5 business days.

## Component XK-7 — Timing Controller
Component XK-7 is a timing controller used exclusively in Product X within this catalog.
Revision history: XK-7 rev C fixed jitter above 40C (see field report Q3).
Do NOT substitute XK-5: pinout differs and thermal envelope is narrower.
