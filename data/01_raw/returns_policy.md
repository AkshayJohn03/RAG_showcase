# Returns & RMA Policy (rev 2026-01)

## Return window
Standard return window: **30 days** from delivery for unopened Product X and
Product Y units. Opened units are covered by warranty instead (36 months).

## RMA process (code RMA-77)
1. Open a ticket quoting RMA-77 and the unit serial.
2. Ship with the original XK-7 controller installed (do not swap parts pre-RMA).
3. Refunds post within 10 business days of warehouse receipt.

## Exclusions
Consumables, expedited shipping fees, and the 12% expedite surcharge on XK-7
orders are non-refundable. Hot-work damage without a SAF-114 permit voids RMA.
