## Hot-work permits
Clause **SAF-114**: Any soldering or rework on Component XK-7 requires a hot-work
permit signed by the shift lead. Permit validity: 8 hours. Fire watch required
for 60 minutes after completion.

Clause SAF-118: General electronics bench work (non-XK-7) requires only standard
ESD controls, no hot-work permit.
